# Castelo do Bode Angler

A pocket field guide to fishing the Castelo do Bode reservoir, Portugal: fishing spots on a satellite map, lure advice by time of day and weather, a day planner and an almanac.

Open on a phone and use "Add to Home Screen" to install it. It works offline after the first visit (live weather needs signal).

Credits: satellite imagery Sentinel-2 cloudless 2024 by EOX (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), weather by Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), map library Leaflet, photos from Wikimedia Commons (credits in the Almanac). Non-commercial.
